## Related Categories
In Naruto, each member belongs to a specific clan. Most of the clans we know of live in the [[Hidden Leaf Village]], so most of them all get along. Each clan has some sort of special ability or power they are known for and usually use in times of battle. Each page will be taking a deeper dive into each individual clan to see what makes that clan special in the Naruto Verse.
