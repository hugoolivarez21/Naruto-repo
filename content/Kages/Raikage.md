# Leader of Kumogakure

The Hidden Cloud Village is similar to the [[Mizukage]] in that they pick the strongest member of the village to lead and protect the village, using the Lightning release. The Raikage is usually the "B" to the Raikages "A". The Raikage picks someone they trust and trains them up to become the next leader, up until the most recent Raikage, who chose his bodyguard instead of his successor/brother. One quote that best describes this role is:
>A Raikage does not run, and a Raikage does not waver. We are the shield and the spear of Kumogakure.
>- A, The Fourth Raikage

## Raikage In Order
1. A (Shodaime)
2. A (Nidaime)
3. A (Sandaime)
4. A (Yondaime)
5. Darui

### Alliances
The most notable alliance with any other Kage is with the Third [[Hokage]], Tobirama. They wanted to sign the Cloud-Leaf Peace Pact, but this got ambushed, which resulted in the death of the Second Raikage. Besides this, Darui now maintains a relationship with the 4 other Kage of the great nations as a result of the Fourth Great Ninja War.
