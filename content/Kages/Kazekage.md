# Leader of Sungakure

In the Hidden Sand Village, the title of Kazekage is given down in inheritance. Every Kazekage comes from the one bloodline and only members of the "Sand Royal Bloodline" if you will. From the bloodline, they are then vetted by the elders to see if the candidates are strong enough to become the Kazekage. This role is given only to the most powerful Kazekage bloodline member of the village. A good quote that describes this role is:
>"As the Kazekage, I had to judge your worth to the village. A leader must weigh the lives of his people against the threat of a monster."
>- Rasa

## Kazekage In Order
1. Reto
2. Shamon
3. Third Kazekage 
4. Rasa
5. Gaara

### Alliances
Besides the Kazekage's long running alliance with the [[Hokage]]'s of the Leaf Village, the Fourth Great Ninja War brought along an alliance with the leaders of each village, specifically the Fourth [[Raikage]], Third [[Tsuchikage]], and the Fifth [[Mizukage]].
