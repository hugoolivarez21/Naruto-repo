## Kirigakure

The Village Hidden in the Mist is led by the [[Mizukage]] and sits in the Land of Water. Every shinobi who lives here specializes in Water Style, and utilize this to some degree in combat. This village is also houses [[Isobu]], Three-Tailed Beast, and [[Saiken]], the Six-Tailed beast. 

## Notable Conflicts
The Hidden Mist Village at a period of time became know for their ruthless graduation classes. They made each student face each other until the death to see who would wouldn't die. The survivors would then go on to graduate and become full fledge shinobi. This only got discontinued after Zabuza Momochi killed an entire graduating class all by himself. Another notable conflict is when Madara [[Uchiha]] took control over the [[Mizukage]] at the time, Yagura (the Three-Tails Junchiriki). During Madara's control, he instated the bloody graduation exams as well as formed a group called the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist. This was a group that was filled of seven of the best shinobi in the village, each wielding a powerful sword which each requires a different skillset to master.
