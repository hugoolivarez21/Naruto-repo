## Konohagakure

The Village Hidden in the Leaf is led by the [[Hokage]], and sits in the Land of Fire. The Hidden Leaf Village is the main focus of the Naruto series, and is the most  village in the verse. Most of the Clans covered in the Naruto series are from here, including the [[Aburame]], [[Hyuga]], [[Uchiha]], and [[Uzumaki]]. This is also where Naruto resides, in turn, where [[Kurama]], the Nine Tailed Fox resides as well. This is also home to the most powerful shinobi in the entire verse including several previous Hokage, such as Naruto, Minato, and Hashirama, as well as some powerful villains like Sasuke, Madara, and Obito. 

## Notable Conflicts
Konoha has seen many conflicts throughout the series of the show. One of the first large scale battles we see is the Attack of the Nine Tails. Obito, a former member of Konoha, took control of the Nine Tails using his sharingan and attacked the village to attempt to destroy the Hidden Leaf and steal the Nine Tails. Another great conflict is when they got attacked by the [[Hidden Sand Village]] during the Chunin Exams in an attempt to destroy the Hidden Leaf. At the time, Oruchimaru killed the current [[Kazekage]] at the time and influenced the Hidden Sand to destroy the leaf. They used Gaara, who is the junchuriki of the the One Tails [[Shukaku]], and was defended by Naruto and Gamabunta. This attempt failed as well. Another big conflict was during the Invasion Arc, Pain attacked the Hidden Leaf and completely destroyed it in an attempt to capture the Nine Tails. Yet again, Naruto defended the Hidden Leaf Village and defeated Pain. Another smaller, but significant conflict was an internal war between the [[Uchiha]] and the Leaf's Elders. After Obito took control of the Nine Tails and attacked the Leaf, many people assumed the Uchiha started the fight due to Kuruma's eyes having the Sharingan, signifying that it was under the control of an Uchiha. This caused alot of internal tension and even caused the Uchiha to plan a revolution against the Leaf, but Itachi ended up killing everyone in his clan except his little brother Sasuke in order to protect the Hidden Leaf. This lead Sasuke down a path of seeking power and ended up training with Oruchimaru, forever competing for power with Naruto.
