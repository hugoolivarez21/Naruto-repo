## Iwagakure

The Village Hidden in the Stone is led by the [[Tsuchikage]], and sits in the Land of Earth. This village is home to two tailed beasts including the Roshi, the Four Tails Jinchuriki for [[Son Goku]], and Han, the Five Tails Jinchuriki for [[Kokuo]].

## Notable Conflicts
There isn't much known about this village besides each Shinobi World War. They have had multiple fights with the [[Hidden Sand Village]] over the years due to their geography, as well as the [[Hidden Leaf Village]]. This village became distrusted throughout the years for their use of the Akatsuki to carry out missions for them, without the need to tie their name to the conflict. One notable figure from the Hidden Stone Village is Deidara, a member of the Akatsuki. Deidara was also a student of the Third Tsuchikage. Deidara went on to commit many crimes for the Akatsuki and became known as highly dangerous, thanks to the Third's teachings.
