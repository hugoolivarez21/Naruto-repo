The site we will be analyzing is https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Narutopedia. Naruto serves as a wikipedia page for all things Naruto, providing information on any topic within Naruto that you would like to know.

The resources being organized in this website range from everything from Characters, Jutsu's, Clans, to a higher level like Movies, Mangas, and the Anime. The best way to move around this site is to hover over the **Setting** dropdown and dig into the hierarchical system.

## Primary Interactions
The primary intereactions supported are **Research, Fact Checking** and **Browsing.** 
### Fact Checking
While exploring the document, you will find many hyperlinks referencing several different parts of either the manga, anime, or movie being referenced in the fact-in-question. For example, the first section includes a brief summary of the show and references [[Hokage]] at the end. You can also click this to cross reference my section to see if I got the information correct or not. 
### Research
If you are interested in a certain group, for example the Akatsuki, you can go to their wiki page and get virtually all the information you are looking for. You can find small things, such as the rings and what significance they played, to larger things such as the orgin stories of each version of the Akatsuki. They also provide hyperlinks to each individual Akatsuki member if you want to perform a deeper dive into their stories.
### Browsing
If you are unsure what you want to look into, their homepage provides a **Random Episode** and **On This Day** section to provide a more casual browsing experience. They also provide a **Community** section to discuss interests within the community or provide friendly banter. 

# Classification System
The classification system used in this website hierarchical. The tree style classification will break categories down as see in our Resources section. This works well with the audience as the audience is usually wanting to go to this site to get deeper about specific Naruto topics. To someone who comes in knowing nothing about Naruto, they can still come into the website to gain a better understanding and not feel confused, as the high level clarification system is worded in a very generic language, and then gets specific based on what you are looking for. # Relationships
The entire wiki heavily relies on hyperlinks throughout the site. Anytime a character or concept is mentioned, there will be a hyperlink to take you to that topic and provide the ability to educate further. There are also infobox's throughout the individual topics as well as a "...more about" section.
