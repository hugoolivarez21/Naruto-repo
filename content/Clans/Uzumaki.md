## The Clan of Life

The Uzumaki clan is one of the most important clans of the Naruto series, as it is of course Naruto's clan. This clan is most known for their massive chakra reserves and their great power. They have notoriously been great healers as well as shinobi. They share close relation to the Senju clan, which is the direct decendents of Ashura Otsutsuki, or the younger child to the Sage of Six Paths. This clan and the Senju clan often married into one another, sharing their powerful DNA. They are most known for their red hair, shown with characters like Kushina, Nagato, and Karin. Not much else is know about this clan compared to the Senju clan, but their importance in the Naruto Verse is felt throughout the entire series. Many of these clan members share the Will of Fire along with the Senju. They are another founding clan of the [[Hidden Leaf Village]]. One notable quote to describe this clan is:
>"Even among other ninja, their chakra is uniquely strong. An Uzumaki can survive injuries that would instantly kill anyone else, and they can even survive the extraction of a Tailed Beast for a short time."
> - Kushina Uzumaki

### Notable Uzumaki Clan Moments
The Uzumaki clan has many notable moments showing off their incredible life force. The biggest display of their chakra reserves is in Naruto's great control of [[Kurama]], the Nine Tails Jinchuriki. Another great display is when Obito extracted [[Kurama]] out of Kushina and Kushina was able to survive that extraction. Nagato, who at this point in the story is a villain, was able to stretch his chakra across 6 deal individual, giving them life, and each of them their own set of jutsu. Karin also had many notable moments of healing, allowing Sasuke to take a bite of her body and regain his own chakra. This clan also created the Reaper Death Seal, which is the most powerful seal known in the Verse.
