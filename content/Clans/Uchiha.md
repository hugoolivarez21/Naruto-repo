## The Sharingan Clan

The Uchiha clan is the single most powerful clan in the entire [[Hidden Leaf Village]] and arguably the entire Naruto Verse. They are the direct decedentd to Indra Otsutski, the first born son of the Sage of Six Paths, or the founding father of the Shinobi. Some Uchiha are so powerful, they can even control [[Kurama]], the most powerful tailed beast behind the [[Ten-Tails]]. This clan is most known for their visual jutsu, the Sharingan. The Sharingan can do many powerful jutsu, such as illusions, eternal flames, teleportation, matter manipulation, mental manipulation, and even the ability to cheat death. This clan is the most feared for their overwhelming power and history of evil figures throughout the story of Naruto. One notable quote that describes this clan is:
> "The Uchiha is a clan that is possessed by evil... They value love above all else, and yet they treat that love with reckless abandon."
>  - Tobirama Senju, The Second [[Hokage]]

### Notable Uchiha Clan Moments
The Uchiha clan is a main focus in the Naruto story, so naturally, they have many standout moments. Some moments are the constants fights between Sasuke and Naruto, fulfilling a Yin and Yang destiny between the 2 offspring of the Sage of Six Paths. Another big moment for Itachi is when he was ordered to kill his entire clan, leaving only Sasuke only alive for the sake of the [[Hidden Leaf Village]]. Another notable moment is the final fight between Sasuke and Itachi, as Itachi fights careful enough to get Sasuke rid of Oruchimaru, who is sealed inside of Sasuke before finally allowing his little brother Sasuke to defeat him in battle and fulfill his wish of vengeance for the Uchiha clan. Madara Uchiha, who is the most powerful Uchiha (behind present day Sasuke) is a character who gets spoken about throughout the entire show. His resurrection during the 4th Great Ninja War Arc brings about his first introduction to battle, really showing his power and what a true Uchiha can really do in battle. This is a clan who has many moments throughout the series, and we are barely scratching the surface.
