## The Bug Clan

The Aburame clan is a clan who's primary weapons consists of bugs and insects. The Aburame clan live in the [[Hidden Leaf Village]] located in the Land of Fire. The Aburame clan have utilized these insects to perform many tasks including recon, poison, chakra control, flight, paralysis, and even suffocation. The name Aburame is short for Aburamushi, which direclty translates to "cockroach". One notable quote which can be interpreted as describing this clan with their relation to bugs is:
>"Never underestimate your opponent, no matter how small they may seem."
> - Shino Aburame

## Notable Clan Members

- Torune Aburame

### Notable Aburame Clan Moments
The Aburame Clan play a small role in the Naruto story but stills hare some big moments. One huge moment for Shino is during the Sasuke Retrieval Arc, Shino pops up unannounced and uses his bugs ability to feed off chakra to win him his fight against his enemy, this is the first time we as an audience see the true ability of the Aburame Clan. Another notable moment is when Torune fights Shisui, a powerful member of the [[Uchiha]] Clan, and manages to touch him with his poisoned arms, which can then poison the recipient. This is notable as Shisui is known as "The Teleporter" due to his use of the Body Flicker technique, making him unbeatable at the least.
