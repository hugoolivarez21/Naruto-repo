## The Byakugan Clan

The Hyuga clan is one of the most powerful clans in Konoha, only second to the [[Uchiha]] Clan. This clan is best known for their Kekkei Genkai, which for the Hyuga's is their Byakugan, or their eyes that allow them to see their opponents chakra points. Another strong ability of this clan lies in their ability to see at greater distances than most, up to 20 kilometers, and up to 360 degrees with only one tiny blind spot in the back of their head. This clan is split into 2 branches, the main branch and the side branch, with the side branch destined to protect the main branch with their lifes. They specialize mainly in Taijutsu due to their speciality being their Gentle Fist technique. One quote that best describes the Hyuga clan is:
>"No matter how good a ninja you are, no one can escape my Byakugan"
> - Hiashi Hyuga

### Notable Hyuga Clan Moments
The Hyuga clan have many notable moments within the Naruto series. One notable moment is the fight between Neji and Hinata, where we as the audience learn the history and politics behind the Hyuga Clan. Another notable moment for Neji is when he saved Hinata by sacrificing himself during the 4th Great Ninja War. Another moment for Hinata is when she went toe-to-toe with Pain during the Invasion Arc to fight while Naruto was down.
