## Related Categories
In Naruto, each [[hidden-villages/index|Hidden Village]] holds their own tailed beast as a result of a peace treaty from Hashirama Senju. The goal of the treaty was for each village to have an equal amount of power so as to deter conflict. Each Tailed Beast comes from the [[Ten-Tails]] and can resummon the Ten Tails if combined. Their hosts are called Jinchuriki and are often seen as the most powerful of their respective village, which Naruto [[Uzumaki]] happens to be. Each Tailed beast has the ability to shoot out a Tailed Beast Bomb, which can be combined into one giant bomb which is powerful blast that can wipe out mountains. They also have the ability to heal their hosts quickly in some mannor, due to their large chakra forms.
