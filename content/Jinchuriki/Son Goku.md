## The Four Tailed Beast

Son Goku's host is Roshi of the [[Hidden Stone Village]]. Fun fact about this pair is this was based off of Dragon Ball, as the creator on the manga respected the creator of Dragon Ball and wanted a way to pay homage in the show. This tailed beast takes on the appearance of a monkey, continuing the Dragon Ball trend. Son Goku is prideful which complements Roshi's stubbornness. Son Goku specializes in Lava release and is highly skilled in Taijutsu, or hand to hand combat. Son Goku ended up being captured by Kisame of the Ataksuki. Not much else is known about this tailed beast as his significance to the story was small.
