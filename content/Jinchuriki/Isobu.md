## The Three Tailed Beast

Isobu's jinchuriki host is Yagura of the [[Hidden Mist Village]]. Yagur, the fourth [[Mizukage]], was also the only jinchuriki who was able to gain the complete trust of Isobu, and in turn had access to Isobu's complete power. Isobu is more timid compared to his tailed beast counterparts. Yagura is also naturally timid as well, but both of their personalities changed when under the control of Madara [[Uchiha]]. Isobu takes on the appearance of a turtle, using its large shell as both an attack and defense mechanism with spikes around its shell and parts of its body. Isobu's possesses a special ability, producing mist that places the user under a genjutsu and makes the victim face their biggest fears. Isobu's only physical weakness is the one opened eye, as it is the only soft part of the beasts exterior. Isobu can also roll into a ball and move around more quickly. Another one of Isobu's special abilities are the ability to create shockwaves and tidal waves, as well as the ability to move dimensions to hide if needed. The Akatsuki were able to capture Isobu  with Deidara and Tobi.
