## The Two Tailed Beast

Matatabi's jinchuriki host is Yugito from the [[Hidden Cloud Village]]. Matatabi's personality is more polite which contradicts Yugito's fierce personality. Matatabi takes on the form of a cat who is made up of all flames. Matatabi uses fire release as and power is shown through its raw strength and agility. Yugito ended up gaining the trust of Matatabi, becoming a perfect junchuriki and utilizing Matatabi's full powers. Matatabi ended up being captured by Akatsuki members Hidan and Kakazu. Not much information is given regarding this tailed beast as its role to the story is minimal.
