## The Ten Tailed Beast

The Ten-Tails is a combination of all tailed beasts. The important holders of this Jinchuriki include Hagaromo Otsutsuki, Obito [[Uchiha]], and Madara Uchiha. The Ten Tails can only be summoned when all of the tailed beasts are combined with the God Tree and is the most powerful beast compared to each tailed beast combined. Hagaromo became the first jinchuriki in order to keep the Ten Tails from causing harm to the world and Obito and Madara became the jinchuriki to further their harm of the world. This beast is capable of performing each type of release and has the ability to absorb jutsu and spit them back as well. Its sole goal is to collect all chakra to summon Kaguya to the world and allow Kaguya to eat the world. This tailed beast was sealed by Naruto [[Uzumaki]], Sasuke Uchiha, and Sakura Haruno.
