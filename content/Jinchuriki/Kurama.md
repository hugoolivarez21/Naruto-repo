## The Nine Tailed Beast

Kurama host is Naruto [[Uzumaki]] from the [[Hidden Leaf Village]]. This tailed beast was interestingly only sealed in members of the Uzumaki family, likely due to their large chakra reserves with this being the most powerful tailed beast. Kurama takes on the appearance of a fox and has attacks similar to how a fox would attack. Naruto was able to gain the trust of Kurama after being able to change Kurama's mind on how cynical the world was. Kurama is naturally more cynical while Naruto is a very optimistic person. Kurama has the ability to create multiple tailed beast bombs simultaneously. Kurama's powers come in its raw strengh that itself possesses as well as the strength it can give its Jinchuriki. It has the ability to create a chakra cloak around its user, creating a mix between a human form with tailed beast chakra. It also has the ability to enter Baryon Mode, which provides immense power for a short period of time, unfortunately killing the tailed beast after use.
