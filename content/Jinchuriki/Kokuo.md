## The Five Tailed Beast

Kokuo's jinchuriki host is Han from the [[Hidden Stone Village]]. Kokuo is a tailed beast who does not like conflict and is more quiet and reserved, much like his host Han. Kokuo takes on the head of a dolphin and the body of a horse. Kokuo's special ability to emit hot steam from its body by overheating its chakra, allowing for incredible strength to flow through itself and its host. This is reflected in its main battle attack being itself ramming into its opponents. It is not know which member of the Akatsuki captured the Five Tails, but speculation seems to point to Pain or Konan, as they were the only members of the Akatsuki at the time.
