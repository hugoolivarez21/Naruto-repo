## The Eight Tailed Beast

Gyuki's jinchuriki host is Killer B from the [[Hidden Cloud Village]]. Killer B was also the only Jinchuriki to be able to befriend Gyuki and use Gyuki's power to the full extent. Gyuki takes the form of an Octopus and his attacks are often octopus themed. Gyuki is known to be a more serious tailed beast which is in contrast to Killer B, who is usually careless and won't stop rapping every change he gets, which annoys Gyuki. The only tailed beast that Gyuki did not like was [[Kurama]], due to the fact that Kurama believed a tailed beasts strength lied in its number of tails. One of Gyuki's abilities is to be able to cut off a limb and replace its owner with that limb as a substitution. That cut off limb will eventually heal itself with time. Gyuki is also skilled in Genjutsu, using his ink to either put enemies in a genjutsu, or blind their enemies and seal them. Gyuki can also use its tails as protection, wrapping them around its host or itself. Gyuki is also special from other tailed beasts with his ability to perform an even greater tailed beast bomb than normal. Gyuki was never captured fully by the Akatsuki, but did give enough chakra with a severed limb to summon the [[Ten-Tails]]. Members who attempted to capture B and Gyuki were Sasuke [[Uchiha]], Suigetsu, Karin [[Uzumaki]], and Kisame.
