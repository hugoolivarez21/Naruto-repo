## One Tailed Beat

Shukaku's jinchuriki host is Gaara of the [[Hidden Sand Village]]. This beast takes on the appearance of a racoon and the attitude of a child. This is the most violent of the tailed beasts as it actively looks for opportunities to kill. At first, Gaara acted this way as well as he was very bloodthirsty, but after fighting Naruto [[Uzumaki]], Gaara changed into a more level headed and planned out individual. Shukaku has curse marks all around his body symbolizing his great defensive and sealing abilities. Shukaku also specializes in sand style, combining it with, wind style, earth style, and magnet style making this jinchuriki the most versatile offensively. This tailed beast also has the ability to take over their host's body when they sleep. Gaara was not able to fully control Shukaku as their ideologies clashed and they did not get along, but towards the end of the show, was able to gain the trust of Shukaku as Gaara attempted to protect Shukaku from getting extracted. Shukaku did end up getting captured by Deidara of the Akatsuki.
