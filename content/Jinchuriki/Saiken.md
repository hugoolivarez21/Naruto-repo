## The Six Tailed Jinchuriki

Saiken's jinchuriki host is Utakata of the [[Hidden Mist Village]]. Saiken takes on the form of a slug and utilizes bubbles for combat. Saiken is a kind beast but will put itself first if the situation calls for it. This matches Utakata's personality of being more calm. Saiken's special ability lies within its slimy substance around its body, allowing it to shoot out corrosive slime and disintegrate opponents in the form of gas or liquid. Saiken was captured by Pain of the Akatsuki. Not much is know of this tailed beast as it is not a focus of the Naruto Verse.
