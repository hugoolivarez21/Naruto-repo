## The Seven Tailed Beast

Chomei's jinchuriki host is Fu from the Village Hidden by a Waterfall. This is the only tailed beast to not have lived within the [[hidden-villages/index|5 Great Nations]]. Chomei often has a care-free, happy personality matching that of its host Fu, loosely based off of the lucky number 7. Chomei's special ability is the ability to blind its opponent using its wings and is the only beast that can fly. Most of Chomei's attacks are based off of bugs, as that is the tailed beast's form. Fu ended up being captured by Akatsuki members Kakuzu and Hidan.
